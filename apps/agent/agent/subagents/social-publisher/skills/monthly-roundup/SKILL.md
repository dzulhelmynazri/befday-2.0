# Monthly Roundup Editorial Skill

Use this skill when drafting monthly birthday cheat sheets, first-of-the-month megathreads, and "Standby Apps" prep lists (e.g., _"Geng lahir bulan November, boleh ready dah nak claim birthday reward korang!!"_ or _"November babies! Don't forget to redeem your birthday treats!"_).

---

## 1. Why This Format Goes Viral

On Threads and X, monthly birthday roundups achieve **80%–100%+ share-to-like ratios** when formatted as **zero-fluff, screenshot-able cheat sheets** rather than marketing paragraphs:

1. **Instant Scan Value**: Readers can scan 10–15 brands in 5 seconds.
2. **Zero Cashier Embarrassment**: Separating `FREE` (100% Free / Confirm Dapat) from `VOUCHER` (Bersyarat / With Purchase) prevents users from walking into a store expecting a free item without knowing a purchase is required.
3. **High DM & Tag Velocity**: People immediately forward the list to friends born in that month or bookmark it for themselves.

---

## 2. Three Proven Viral Threads Archetypes

Select one of these 3 archetypes (or combine them across a `1/3`, `2/3`, `3/3` thread):

### Archetype A: The "1-Liner Numbered Stack" (`1/3` Thread)

Best for maximum reach, likes, and swipe-throughs.

**Post 1/3 (Top Crowd Favorites)**:

```text
Geng lahir bulan [Month], boleh ready dah nak claim birthday reward korang!! 🎂✨
(Save siap-siap / tag member lahir bulan [Month]!)

1. 🍦 FamilyMart — FREE Sofuto (app)
2. 🍨 Baskin Robbins — FREE single scoop (app)
3. 🥤 Boost Juice — FREE birthday smoothie (Vibe app)
4. 🍱 Bungkus Kaw Kaw — FREE Nasi Lemak + drink (app)
5. 🧋 Gong Cha — Free drink voucher (app)
6. ☕ Coffee Bean — FREE slice cake (with 1 drink purchase)
7. 🍰 ZUS Coffee — Buy 1 Free 1 cake (app)
8. 🍗 Nando's — FREE birthday treat (app)
9. 🍣 Sushi King — FREE birthday dessert (app)
10. 🎬 GSC Cinemas — 2 FREE movie tickets (member)

(Sambungan part 2/3 bawah 👇)
```

**Post 2/3 (More Perks + Claim Mechanics)**:

- Continue numbering `11.` to `20.` with exact 2–4 word conditions.

**Post 3/3 (The 30-Day App Warning + Full Directory CTA)**:

```text

Kebanyakan app (FamilyMart, Starbucks, CBTL, Boost) kena register sekurang-kurangnya 30 hari sebelum bulan birthday!

Nak tengok full list 100+ birthday freebies siap dengan link download app & syarat penuh?
Terus pergi kat befday.com (link in bio) 🎁
```

## 3. Execution Workflow

1. **Optional Trend Check (Treg MCP)**: Search `site:threads.net "birthday" "lahir bulan"` via Treg to see if any new merchant perk is trending this week.
2. **Query Database (`get_brand_perks`)**: Fetch both `badge: "FREE"` and `badge: "VOUCHER"` perks with `status: "PUBLISHED"` so every item on the list is 100% verified.
3. **Draft & Schedule (`schedule_social_post`)**: Format using one of the 3 archetypes above and schedule across `threads`, `facebook_page`, `instagram`, and `linkedin`.
