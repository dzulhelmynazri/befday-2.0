# Short-Form Video Script Skill

Use this skill when tasked with creating short-form video scripts (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) for single-brand hacks or fast-paced birthday deal countdowns.

---

## 1. Video Guidelines (15–30 Seconds)

Short-form algorithms prioritize **watch time and completion rate**. Every script must eliminate slow intros and deliver immediate value within the first 3 seconds.

---

## 2. Standard 3-Part Video Formula

### Part 1: The 3-Second Hook (0:00–0:03)

- **Goal**: Stop the scroll with a pattern-interrupt or contrarian statement.
- **Visual**: Creator speaking directly to camera while pointing to an app or brand logo.
- **Hook Ideas**:
  - _“Do NOT buy your own coffee on your birthday in Malaysia...”_
  - _“If your birthday is this month, here is how to get 100% free food...”_
  - _“The secret birthday hack that [Brand] doesn't advertise...”_

### Part 2: The Core Reveal & Proof (0:03–0:20)

- **Visual**: Screen recording of the app wallet or B-roll of the physical treat.
- **On-Screen Text**: Bold, high-contrast captions highlighting key terms (_FREE NO PURCHASE_, _VALID ALL MONTH_).
- **Spoken Audio**:
  - State the exact reward.
  - State the registration cut-off (_“You MUST download their app at least 30 days before your birth month”_).
  - Clarify spend condition (_“It’s totally free, no purchase required”_ or _“You just need to buy one drink”_).

### Part 3: The Viral Call to Action (0:20–0:30)

- **Spoken Audio**:
  - _“Send this to your friend celebrating a birthday this month so they don't miss out.”_
  - _“Check the link in bio for the complete list of 100+ freebies.”_
- **On-Screen Text**: _"Share with a [Month] baby! 🎂"_

---

## 3. Output Format Template

When generating a video script, output this structured format:

```markdown
### 📱 Video Concept: [Brand / Topic] Birthday Hack

- **Target Platform**: TikTok / Instagram Reels
- **Target Duration**: 20–25 seconds
- **Audio Recommendation**: Trending upbeat background audio (low volume)

---

| Timestamp | [Visual Direction] | [Spoken Voiceover] | [On-Screen Text] |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **0:00–0:03** | Close-up face to camera, pointing to logo | _"Stop scrolling if your birthday is this month..."_ | STOP SCROLLING 🚨 |
| **0:03–0:12** | Screen recording of app voucher | _"[Brand] literally gives you a free [Perk] during your birthday month."_ | FREE [PERK] 🎁 |
| **0:12–0:20** | Show receipt / redemption barcode | _"All you need to do is register their app 30 days early. No purchase needed."_ | 100% FREE • NO SPEND |
| **0:20–0:25** | Point to bio / comment prompt | _"Send this to someone born this month! Full list in bio."_ | SHARE TO A BIRTHDAY FRIEND 📲 |

---

### 📝 Accompanying Video Caption

_Short punchy caption with 3–5 viral hashtags for scheduling:_ Did you know about this [Brand] birthday hack? 🎁 Make sure you sign up at least 30 days before! Full guide in bio. #birthdayfreebies #malaysiahacks #fooddeals #klfoodie #befday
```
