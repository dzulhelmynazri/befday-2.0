# Brand Deep-Dive Editorial Skill

Use this skill when tasked with writing a dedicated, single-merchant birthday guide (e.g., *"Starbucks Birthday Reward: How to Claim Your Free Cake (2026)"*, *"FamilyMart Free Birthday Sofuto Guide"*, or *"How to Get Free Movie Tickets at GSC on Your Birthday"*).

---

## 1. Intent & Structure

A brand deep-dive targets high-intent, transactional queries (e.g., _"[Brand] birthday reward terms"_, _"how to claim [brand] birthday voucher"_). Readers and AI assistants want zero fluff—just the exact rules, eligibility cut-offs, gotchas, and links.

### Document Hierarchy:

```markdown
1. H1 Title & Meta Description (Brand name, exact reward, current year)
2. Standalone Answer Capsule (40–60 words, direct reward & condition summary)
3. Quick Facts Box (Reward badge, validity, app name, minimum spend)
4. Step-by-Step Claim Walkthrough (Numbered 1-4 with exact app steps)
5. Fine Print & Gotchas (Dine-in only, qualifying purchase, registration cut-off)
6. Official Action Links (App Store, Play Store, Store Locator)
7. Brand-Specific FAQs (3–4 hyper-targeted questions)
```

---

## 2. Section Playbook

### A. Title & Slug
- **Title Formula**: `[Brand Name] Birthday Reward ([Year]): How to Claim Your [Perk Name]`
- **Slug**: `[brand-slug]-birthday-reward-[year]` (e.g., `starbucks-birthday-reward-2026`)
- **Meta Description**: 140–160 characters stating the exact reward, eligibility criteria, and required app.

### B. Standalone Answer Capsule (40–60 words)
- **Position**: Immediately beneath the H1 title.
- **Template**:
  > *"[Brand Name] offers [Reward description] for loyalty members during their birthday [validity period, e.g. month/day]. To claim, members must register on the official [App Name] at least [X days, e.g. 30 days] before their birth date. [Mention if purchase is required: e.g. The reward is 100% free with no purchase needed / Requires purchasing any handcrafted beverage]."*

### C. Quick Facts Table
Provide a clear Markdown summary table upfront:

| Detail | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Birthday Reward** | [Exact perk name, e.g., Free slice of cake / Free Sofuto] |
| **Reward Type** | `FREE` (No purchase required) or `VOUCHER` (Requires spend) |
| **Validity Window** | [e.g., Entire birthday month / Within 7 days of birthday] |
| **Loyalty Program** | [Official loyalty program name] |
| **Claim Channel** | [Mobile App / Counter barcode / Physical ID] |
| **Advance Sign-Up** | [e.g. Must register 1 month prior / Immediate upon signup] |

### D. Step-by-Step Claim Walkthrough
Provide numbered, unambiguous steps:
1. **Download & Register**: Name the exact mobile app on iOS and Android. Specify the birthdate entry requirement.
2. **Timing / Cut-off**: Explain when the voucher or reward drops into the app wallet (e.g., on the 1st day of the birth month).
3. **In-Store or Online Redemption**: Step-by-step instructions on presenting the barcode at the cash register or applying the voucher during mobile checkout.
4. **Verification**: State what physical proof is required (e.g., physical IC/passport or solely the in-app member QR code).

### E. Fine Print, Terms & Gotchas
Bullet points covering critical details that trip users up:
- Minimum spend or qualifying item required (if `VOUCHER`).
- Dine-in vs takeaway restrictions.
- Excluded airport, resort, or franchise outlets.
- Expiration policy (cannot be extended).

### F. Official App & Store Links
Render clean links to official channels:
- `[Download for iOS (App Store)](url)`
- `[Download for Android (Google Play)](url)`
- `[Find Nearest Store (Google Maps)](url)`

### G. Brand-Specific FAQs
Answer 3–4 hyper-specific questions uncovered via Treg search:
- *"Can I claim my [Brand] birthday treat if I register today on my birthday?"*
- *"Can I combine my birthday reward with ongoing discount promos or vouchers?"*
- *"Is the birthday treat available for delivery orders?"*
